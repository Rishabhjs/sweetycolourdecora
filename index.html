Questions Worth Answering

Everything you need to know about our turnkey execution, AI design estimation, and construction management.

In traditional construction, separate painters, marble masons, ceiling carpenters, and electricians constantly blame each other for delays and surface defects. Sweety Colour Decora manages all trades under one master contract with unified project management, daily supervision, and verified sequencing. If a surface needs rework, we take full accountability.
Unlike generic AI tools, our cost engine is calibrated against real 2026 contractor unit rates across 9 major Indian cities (Ahmedabad, Surat, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata). It calculates actual square-footage scope across materials (Italian marble, gypsum boards, paints, tiles) and includes a standard 10% contingency reserve.
Yes, absolutely. Over 60% of our projects are executed in direct collaboration with leading architectural studios. We translate CAD blueprints, elevation drawings, and 3D renders into physical reality with laser-guided ±1mm tolerances and itemized milestone tenders.
Our on-site supervisors upload high-resolution timestamped site photos, daily trade workforce headcounts, and material delivery logs directly to Construction OS. Clients and architects can check progress from their phone at any hour and approve milestone billing stages only when verified.
For projects in Gujarat and Maharashtra, our site engineers conduct physical laser surveys within 24 to 48 hours of enquiry. A comprehensive, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with fixed material specifications and trade milestone schedules is delivered within 3 business days.
